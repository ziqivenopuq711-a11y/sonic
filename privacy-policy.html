1. Introduction

Welcome to OndosNews. This Privacy Policy describes how OndosNews ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your personal information when you visit our website at ondosnews.com (the "Site"), subscribe to our newsletter, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using OndosNews, you consent to the data practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms herein, please discontinue use of our Site. We are committed to responsible data stewardship and full compliance with applicable privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and emerging digital privacy frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Information you provide directly: Name, email address, organization, and message content submitted through our contact form or newsletter sign-up.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time on site, and other standard web analytics data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Session identifiers, preference cookies, and analytics cookies as described in Section 4.
  • Communications data: Any messages or correspondence you send to our editorial or support team.

We do not collect financial information, payment card data, or cryptocurrency wallet addresses through our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our editorial content and site functionality
  • To send newsletters and editorial updates to subscribers who have opted in
  • To respond to inquiries, press release submissions, and partnership proposals
  • To analyze site traffic patterns and optimize the reader experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes under any circumstances.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

OndosNews uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect analytical data. The categories of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for core site functionality such as session management and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to measure page views, traffic sources, and user behavior in aggregate (e.g., via Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled).
  • Preference cookies: Store your display preferences such as dark mode settings across sessions.

You may manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to access our content.

5. Third-Party Services

OndosNews uses a limited number of third-party services in the operation of our Site. These may include analytics providers, email distribution platforms, and content delivery networks. Each third-party processor is bound by a data processing agreement that restricts the use of your data to the specific purpose for which it was shared.

Our Site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. Newsletter subscriber information is retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained in anonymized form for up to 26 months.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right of Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your personal data for certain purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@ondosnews.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption, access controls, and regular security audits to protect personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your devices.

9. Children's Privacy

OndosNews is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify registered newsletter subscribers by email. Continued use of OndosNews after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
