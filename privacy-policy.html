Privacy Policy

CakeSwap is committed to protecting the privacy of our readers, contributors, and business contacts. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, the legal principles that guide our decisions, and the choices available to users. We designed this page to be readable as well as comprehensive because privacy should be understandable, not hidden behind unnecessary complexity. By using CakeSwap, you acknowledge the practices described here and the safeguards we apply to support responsible data handling.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to CakeSwap's website, editorial communications, partnership outreach, and related digital services. It covers personal data that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual, including information provided directly by users and certain technical data collected through site operation. We review our privacy practices regularly to ensure they remain aligned with evolving legal requirements, reader expectations, and the practical realities of running a modern digital publication focused on crypto and financial technology.

Our goal is to collect only the information necessary to operate the site effectively, communicate with users responsibly, and improve the relevance and security of our services. We do not frame data collection as a blank check. Instead, we aim for proportionality, meaning the amount and sensitivity of data handled should match the legitimate purpose behind its use. That principle informs how CakeSwap designs forms, manages access, and evaluates third-party service relationships.

Data Collection

CakeSwap may collect information that users provide voluntarily, such as names, email addresses, company details, and message contents submitted through contact forms, press release inquiries, newsletter subscriptions, or partnership discussions. We may also receive basic technical information automatically when the site is accessed, including browser type, device characteristics, referring pages, approximate location derived from IP data, and usage metrics related to page views and interaction patterns. This information helps us maintain site functionality, monitor performance, and improve editorial delivery.

Where appropriate, we minimize or aggregate technical data to reduce privacy impact. We also seek to avoid collecting special category data or unnecessary sensitive information through our standard workflows. If a user chooses to include additional personal information in a message or submission, we handle it in accordance with this policy and limit access to those with a legitimate business or editorial need to review it.

How We Use Data

We use personal data to respond to inquiries, review submissions, publish approved materials, deliver requested communications, secure the website, analyze readership trends, and improve the relevance of our content and services. In some cases, processing is necessary to perform a requested action, such as replying to a contact form or evaluating a press release for publication. In other cases, processing is based on legitimate interests, including preventing abuse, maintaining platform integrity, and understanding how readers engage with our coverage so we can improve the publication over time.

CakeSwap does not sell personal data in the ordinary course of business. If we work with service providers that support hosting, analytics, communications, or operations, they are expected to handle information only for authorized purposes and with appropriate safeguards. We retain data only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law, dispute management, or security needs.

Cookies Policy

CakeSwap may use cookies and similar technologies to support core site performance, remember user preferences, measure audience engagement, and understand how readers navigate our pages. Some cookies are strictly necessary for technical operation, while others help us evaluate traffic patterns and optimize content presentation. Users can manage cookie preferences through browser settings, and many browsers allow individuals to block or delete cookies entirely, although doing so may affect site functionality or personalization.

We aim to use cookies in a way that is proportionate and transparent. Where consent is required by applicable law, CakeSwap will seek that consent before deploying non-essential technologies. We encourage users to review their browser controls and privacy settings regularly so they can make choices that match their preferences around tracking, convenience, and site performance.

User Rights

Depending on the user's location, applicable law may provide rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. CakeSwap respects these rights and will evaluate requests in good faith, subject to verification and any lawful limitations. Users who want to exercise a privacy right should contact us with enough information for us to identify the relevant records and understand the scope of the request. We may need to retain limited information when required for legal compliance, security, or legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

For readers in jurisdictions covered by GDPR or similar frameworks, CakeSwap strives to provide information about processing purposes, retention logic, and complaint avenues in a clear and usable format. We believe privacy rights should be practical, not theoretical, and we work to make the request process understandable for both casual readers and professional contacts.

Contact for Privacy Matters

Questions about this policy, data handling practices, or privacy-related concerns may be directed to privacy@cakeswapnews.com. Users may also contact the editorial team through the contact page if they need help routing a request. When writing to us, please describe the issue clearly and provide any relevant context, such as the email address used for a submission or the date of a prior interaction. That helps CakeSwap respond more efficiently and accurately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or product changes. When updates are material, we will revise the page content and effective framing accordingly. Continued use of CakeSwap after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy to the extent permitted by law, though users always remain free to discontinue use if they disagree with the changes.
