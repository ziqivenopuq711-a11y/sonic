1. Introduction

Welcome to CakeCoin. This Privacy Policy has been prepared by CakeCoin Media Ltd. ("CakeCoin," "we," "us," or "our") to explain how we handle personal information collected from visitors to our website at cakecoin.news and any associated subdomains or services (collectively, the "Platform"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and processing your data in a transparent, lawful, and responsible manner consistent with applicable data protection legislation including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

By accessing or using the CakeCoin Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms described herein, please discontinue use of our Platform immediately. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time, and significant changes will be communicated via a prominent notice on our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information gathered automatically through your use of the Platform. The categories of personal information we may collect include:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Name, email address, and any other details you voluntarily provide when subscribing to our newsletter, submitting a contact form, or creating an account.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Platform, including pages visited, time spent on each page, links clicked, referral sources, and search queries entered.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution, and cookie identifiers assigned to your browser session.
  • Communication Data: The content of messages you send to us via email or contact forms, including any attachments provided.
  • Marketing Preferences: Your preferences regarding receiving marketing communications from CakeCoin and third-party partners.

3. How We Use Your Information

CakeCoin processes your personal data for specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes. We do not use your data for purposes incompatible with those stated below:

  • To deliver and improve the content, functionality, and performance of the Platform.
  • To send editorial newsletters, breaking news alerts, and market analysis reports to subscribers who have opted in to receive such communications.
  • To respond to inquiries, support requests, press release submissions, and feedback sent through our contact channels.
  • To analyze aggregate audience behavior and preferences to inform our editorial strategy and improve user experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations, including regulatory requests and court orders.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraudulent activity, or violations of our Terms of Use.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

CakeCoin uses cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect analytical information about Platform usage. Cookies are small data files stored in your browser that enable us to recognize your preferences across sessions.

We deploy three categories of cookies: (1) essential cookies required for core Platform functionality; (2) analytical cookies from providers such as Google Analytics that help us understand traffic patterns and reader behavior in aggregate; and (3) advertising cookies that enable relevant content promotion on third-party networks. You may configure your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time, although doing so may impair certain Platform features.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

CakeCoin does not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data in limited, defined circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted vendors who assist in operating the Platform, including email delivery services, analytics providers, and cloud hosting companies, under contractual obligations to protect your information.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to notice to affected users.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data under applicable law. These may include the right to access a copy of your data, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@cakecoin.news.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit using TLS, access controls limiting data availability to authorized personnel, and regular security reviews of our infrastructure. However, no internet transmission or digital storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, and you share information with us at your own risk.
