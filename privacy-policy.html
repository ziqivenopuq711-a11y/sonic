Data Collection and Usage Policy

LrcNews is committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you access our website, how that information is used, and the circumstances under which it may be shared with third parties. When you visit LrcNews, we may collect certain non-personally identifiable information automatically through standard web server logs, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the pages you visit on our site. This data is used solely to analyze site traffic patterns, improve our content offerings, and ensure the technical performance of our platform. If you voluntarily submit information to us — for example by subscribing to our newsletter, submitting a news tip, or filling out a contact form — we collect the personal information you provide, such as your name and email address, for the specific purpose indicated at the time of collection. LrcNews does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third-party marketers. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

Cookie Policy and User Tracking

LrcNews uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site usage, and support our editorial and commercial operations. Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your web browser when you visit our site. We use several categories of cookies: strictly necessary cookies that enable core site functionality such as page navigation and secure access; performance and analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our content, which articles are most popular, and how users navigate between pages; and functionality cookies that remember your preferences such as display settings and language selection. We may also use third-party analytics services to aggregate and analyze usage data in anonymized form. You have the right to control cookie usage through your browser settings, and most modern browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some features on the LrcNews platform. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

User Rights and Data Protection

LrcNews respects and upholds the data rights of our users in accordance with applicable privacy legislation, including the General Data Protection Regulation where it applies to users in the European Economic Area, and similar statutes in other jurisdictions. You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate data, to request the deletion of your personal data where there is no legitimate legal basis for its continued retention, and to object to or restrict certain types of data processing. If you submitted personal information through our contact form, newsletter subscription, or other interactive features of the LrcNews platform, you may contact us at any time to exercise these rights. We will respond to all valid data rights requests within the timeframes required by applicable law. LrcNews implements appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, destruction, or alteration. While we take data security seriously, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we encourage users to take appropriate precautions when sharing personal information online.
