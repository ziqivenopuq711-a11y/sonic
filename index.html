Tokenization Standards: Ondo Joins DTCC Working Group to Transform U.S. Capital Markets

Ondo Finance DTCC Tokenization Capital Markets RWA

Ondo Finance has officially joined the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) tokenization working group, marking a pivotal step toward reshaping how U.S. capital markets operate in the era of blockchain technology. The partnership underscores growing institutional confidence in on-chain financial infrastructure and the increasing push to establish universal standards for tokenized real-world assets (RWAs).

What This Means for Capital Markets

The DTCC, which processes trillions of dollars in securities transactions each year, has been actively exploring how distributed ledger technology can modernize post-trade settlement, reduce counterparty risk, and improve liquidity across asset classes. By bringing Ondo Finance into its working group, the DTCC signals a readiness to engage directly with DeFi-native protocols that have proven track records in compliant tokenization of U.S. Treasury bonds and money-market instruments.

Ondo's flagship products — OUSG and USDY — have attracted hundreds of millions in TVL by offering institutional and retail investors on-chain exposure to short-term U.S. government securities. The firm's inclusion in the DTCC working group is expected to inform how future tokenization frameworks address ownership transfer, custody, compliance, and interoperability with traditional settlement rails.

Key Objectives of the Working Group

  • Define interoperability standards between blockchain networks and legacy clearing systems
  • Establish compliance frameworks for tokenized securities under SEC and FINRA guidelines
  • Evaluate atomic settlement models that eliminate T+2 latency in equity and bond markets
  • Create guidelines for investor protection and anti-money-laundering controls in on-chain markets
"Joining the DTCC working group is a natural extension of our mission to bridge traditional finance and blockchain. We believe standards developed here will form the backbone of institutional DeFi for the next decade." — Ondo Finance spokesperson

Institutional Momentum Behind Tokenization

The DTCC initiative coincides with a broader wave of institutional investment in tokenization. BlackRock's BUIDL fund on Ethereum, Franklin Templeton's on-chain money market product, and JPMorgan's Onyx platform are all racing to capture a market that analysts at Boston Consulting Group estimate could reach $16 trillion by 2030. Standard-setting by bodies like the DTCC is crucial to ensure these offerings can seamlessly integrate with existing brokerage, prime custody, and clearinghouse infrastructure without regulatory ambiguity.

For Ondo Finance specifically, participation in the working group also enhances its credibility as a regulated, compliance-first operator. The project has already obtained Money Services Business licenses and maintains close dialogue with U.S. regulators, positioning it well for the anticipated rulemaking that will govern tokenized securities.

What Comes Next

Industry observers expect the DTCC working group to publish a preliminary framework document outlining tokenization standards in the second half of 2026. This document is expected to address on-chain identity verification, transfer agent roles for tokenized equities, and smart contract audit requirements. Ondo's technical team is anticipated to contribute significantly to the sections covering programmable compliance and automated corporate actions such as dividend distributions and proxy voting on-chain.

The collaboration between a legacy market-structure giant like the DTCC and a frontier DeFi protocol like Ondo Finance reflects how rapidly the boundaries between traditional finance and blockchain are dissolving. As tokenized assets grow in volume and diversity, the standards being written today will define the rules of engagement for the global capital markets of tomorrow.
