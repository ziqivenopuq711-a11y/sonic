Ondo Finance May 10, 2026 FEATURED
Ondo Finance Joins DTCC Tokenization Working Group to Advance U.S. Capital Market Infrastructure Standards

New York, May 10, 2026 — Ondo Finance, the leading provider of institutional-grade tokenized real-world assets, today announced its formal membership in the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) Tokenization Working Group. The working group, comprising major financial institutions and blockchain innovators, is tasked with developing interoperability and compliance standards for tokenized securities in U.S. capital markets.

Ondo's membership reflects the firm's position as a market leader in compliant on-chain financial products, with its OUSG and USDY instruments collectively managing over $700 million in tokenized U.S. government securities. The company's participation is expected to contribute practical protocol design insights to the working group's standards development process, particularly around automated compliance, cross-chain interoperability, and programmable corporate actions.

"We are honored to join the DTCC working group alongside some of the most influential institutions in global capital markets," said the Chief Executive Officer of Ondo Finance. "Establishing robust tokenization standards is not merely a technical exercise — it is foundational infrastructure that will determine how efficiently the next generation of on-chain capital markets can scale."

The working group's first draft of proposed tokenization standards is expected to be published for public comment by Q3 2026, with final guidelines anticipated in early 2027.

Chainlink Labs May 9, 2026
Chainlink Expands Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol to 12 New Blockchain Networks

San Francisco, May 9, 2026 — Chainlink Labs today announced the extension of its Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) to 12 additional blockchain networks, including Aptos, Sui, TON, and several permissioned enterprise chains operated by major financial institutions. The expansion brings the total number of CCIP-connected chains to 34, reinforcing Chainlink's position as the de facto standard for secure cross-chain messaging in institutional DeFi. Tokenized asset issuers using CCIP can now enable their instruments to be transferred and used as collateral across a significantly broader set of networks without sacrificing auditability or compliance.

Circle Internet Financial May 8, 2026
Circle Launches USDC Business Accounts for Mid-Market Enterprises Seeking Stablecoin Treasury Management

Boston, May 8, 2026 — Circle Internet Financial announced the launch of Circle Business Accounts, a treasury management solution enabling mid-market companies to hold, send, and receive USDC directly through a compliant custodial interface. The product integrates with leading ERP systems including SAP and QuickBooks, allowing finance teams to manage stablecoin cash flows alongside traditional fiat operations. Interest-bearing USDC products yielding 4.5% annually are also available to eligible clients. The launch comes as regulatory clarity from the U.S. Stablecoin Act has expanded corporate appetite for on-chain dollar instruments.

Coinbase May 7, 2026
Coinbase Institutional Introduces Prime Brokerage Services for Tokenized Real-World Assets

New York, May 7, 2026 — Coinbase Institutional today launched dedicated prime brokerage services for tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), enabling institutional clients to custody, finance, and trade tokenized Treasuries, private credit instruments, and real estate tokens through a single regulated interface. The service supports assets issued on Ethereum, Base, and Solana, with planned support for additional chains. Initial client onboarding is restricted to qualified institutional investors with minimum AUM of $100 million. The move positions Coinbase as a primary infrastructure provider for the rapidly expanding institutional RWA market, currently estimated at over $15 billion globally.

Fireblocks May 6, 2026
Fireblocks Announces $200M Series F Funding Round to Accelerate Tokenization Infrastructure Globally

Tel Aviv, May 6, 2026 — Fireblocks, the leading digital asset custody and transfer infrastructure provider, announced the closing of a $200 million Series F funding round led by General Atlantic, with participation from existing investors including Sequoia, Paradigm, and Coatue. The capital will be deployed to expand Fireblocks' tokenization platform capabilities, double its enterprise sales team, and accelerate compliance certifications for regulated markets in Japan, Singapore, and the UAE. The company now serves over 1,500 institutional clients, including banks, exchanges, and asset managers, collectively managing trillions in digital asset transactions annually.
