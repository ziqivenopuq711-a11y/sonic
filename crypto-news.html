Ondo Finance Partners with DTCC to Forge Tokenization Standards for U.S. Capital Markets

In a development that signals the deepening integration of blockchain technology with traditional finance, Ondo Finance has been formally admitted to the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation's newly established tokenization working group. The initiative brings together legacy financial infrastructure providers and DeFi innovators to establish binding interoperability standards for tokenized securities.

Ondo's inclusion is significant because the firm manages one of the largest on-chain portfolios of tokenized U.S. Treasuries, with TVL exceeding $700 million across its OUSG and USDY products. The DTCC, which clears and settles approximately $2.5 quadrillion annually, views the partnership as a critical step toward enabling programmable settlement on distributed ledger networks.

  • DTCC and Ondo Finance to co-develop cross-chain settlement standards for tokenized equities
  • Working group includes Goldman Sachs, Fidelity Digital Assets, and Fireblocks as additional members
  • First draft of proposed standards expected by Q3 2026
  • ONDO token surged 5.1% on announcement, reaching a 90-day high

The collaboration underscores a broader trend of regulated DeFi protocols earning seats at the table where market structure decisions are made. For investors tracking the real-world asset tokenization narrative, this development reinforces the long-term thesis that compliant on-chain finance will eventually absorb significant market share from legacy T+2 settlement systems.
