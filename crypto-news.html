Ethereum's Pectra Upgrade Delivers 40% Gas Cost Reduction Across All L2 Networks

The successful deployment of Ethereum's Pectra hard fork has delivered measurable improvements to the network's throughput and cost efficiency, with average transaction fees on popular Layer 2 networks like Arbitrum and Optimism falling by approximately 40%. The upgrade introduces EIP-7702, enabling externally owned accounts to temporarily behave as smart contracts, dramatically simplifying user experience for wallet abstraction. Developers building on Ethereum's L2 ecosystem report significantly improved developer ergonomics and reduced operational overhead.
