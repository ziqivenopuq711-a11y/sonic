Reach Out

Contact CakeSwap

CakeSwap welcomes messages from readers, protocol teams, researchers, and potential partners who want to connect with our editorial operation. Whether you have a developing news tip, an upcoming product announcement, or a strategic idea for collaboration, the fastest way to get our attention is to send concise information with clear context. This page explains how to contact the newsroom, what details help us respond efficiently, and how we handle partnership and submission inquiries.

Contact Form

Contact Information

Our editorial team can be reached for general inquiries at contact@cakeswapnews.com, while time-sensitive story tips should be directed to newsroom@cakeswapnews.com. Project teams seeking press release placement can write to press@cakeswapnews.com, and partnership discussions may be sent to partnerships@cakeswapnews.com. We monitor these channels throughout the business week and prioritize messages that include enough detail for us to assess urgency, credibility, and next steps without requesting multiple rounds of clarification.

When contacting CakeSwap, please identify the purpose of your message in the subject line and include any relevant links, supporting documents, or public references in the body. This helps us route inquiries quickly and reduces delays. If you are sharing material under embargo, state the embargo time clearly and note the time zone. Our team values concise, complete communication because it lets us focus on evaluating the substance of your request rather than sorting through missing information.

Submission Guidelines

For news tips and contributed information, accuracy is the top priority. Please include primary sources whenever possible, such as governance proposals, public statements, transaction references, regulatory filings, or official documentation. Anonymous claims without verifiable support are unlikely to be published, especially if they involve accusations, sensitive disclosures, or market-moving assertions. We welcome thoughtful outreach from builders and researchers, but we expect submissions to respect the same standards of clarity and accountability that guide our editorial work.

Press release submissions should include the company or protocol name, a short summary, publication timing, media contact information, and any relevant asset links. If the announcement references users, funding, or performance statistics, provide a source or explanation for those figures. The more transparent the submission, the faster our editorial and partnerships teams can determine whether it fits CakeSwap's publishing standards.

Partnership Inquiries

CakeSwap works with select partners on sponsored content, distribution packages, event visibility, and strategic campaign support that aligns with our audience. We are most interested in collaborations that educate readers, surface useful products, or add clarity to an important market discussion. Requests that focus only on short-term hype are less likely to be a fit because our audience responds best to material that offers practical value and credible information.

If you are reaching out about a partnership, include your objectives, target audience, timeline, and any budget or distribution requirements you already know. This allows our team to assess alignment quickly and suggest the most relevant format. We aim to build partnerships that support both project visibility and reader trust, which means transparency, accurate messaging, and clear expectations from the beginning.
