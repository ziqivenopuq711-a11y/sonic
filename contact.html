Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a press release for publication?

Use the contact form above, select "Press Release Submission" from the subject dropdown, and attach your press release as a PDF or paste the text directly into the message field. Our editorial team reviews all submissions within 48 hours.

Does CakeCoin offer sponsored content or native advertising?

Yes. We offer clearly labeled sponsored article placements, newsletter sponsorships, and banner advertising. Select "Advertising & Partnerships" in the contact form to receive our current media kit and rate card.

How can I request a correction to a published article?

We take factual accuracy seriously and publish corrections promptly. Please use the contact form, select "Correction Request," and provide the article URL along with the specific inaccuracy and supporting evidence.
